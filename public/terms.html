The service

ReplyLo automates replies to Instagram comments and sends direct messages on your behalf, using Meta's official APIs and the permissions you grant. We provide the software "as is" and aim for high availability but do not guarantee uninterrupted service.

Your responsibilities

You must follow the Instagram Platform Terms and Community Guidelines with the content you automate. Do not use ReplyLo for spam, scams, or messaging people who did not request it. You are responsible for the DM text and links you configure. Accounts that abuse Meta's messaging limits may be suspended by Meta or by us.

Plans and limits

ReplyLo uses prepaid passes. Each pass (Starter, Growth or Pro) lasts 30 days and gives you a reply allowance per post (50, 100 or 250 replies) across up to 15 posts per period. The free plan includes 1 connected account, 3 keyword rules, 20 replies per post and 2 posts per 30-day period. Trials use Growth limits until they expire. If a reply cannot be delivered, the unit is refunded automatically. You can add 100 extra replies to any single post with a top-up.

Refunds and renewals

Passes and top-ups are prepaid and never renew automatically — when a pass expires your account drops to the free limits until you choose to buy again. Passes are non-refundable once activated, except where required by law; if a billing error charges you twice for the same order we will refund the duplicate in full. Top-ups applied to a post are consumed as replies are sent and are non-refundable. Unused replies do not carry over after your 30-day period ends. If you believe a charge was wrong, contact us and we will make it right.

Availability and Meta changes

ReplyLo depends on Meta's APIs. Rate limits, permission reviews, or API changes on Meta's side can affect delivery. Where Meta blocks a message, we cannot force it through.

Termination

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from the dashboard. We may suspend accounts that violate these terms or Meta's platform policies.

Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, our total liability is limited to the amount you paid us in the 12 months before the claim, and we are not liable for lost profits or for actions taken by Meta on your account.
