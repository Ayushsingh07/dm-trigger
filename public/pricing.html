How counting works

1 · Per post

Each post you run gets its own reply allowance (e.g. 100 on Growth). When one post's replies hit its cap, only that post pauses — your other posts keep replying.

2 · Per 30-day period

A pass covers up to 15 different posts in a 30-day period. Counters reset when your period renews, so a post that used everything gets a fresh allowance.

3 · Failures aren't charged

If a reply can't be delivered, the unit is refunded automatically. You only pay for replies that actually reach the commenter.

All prices in INR, inclusive of applicable taxes. Passes never auto-renew — see our terms.
