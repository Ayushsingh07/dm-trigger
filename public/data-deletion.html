Data deletion

How to get your data removed from ReplyFlow — permanently, usually within 24 hours.

1. I'm a ReplyFlow customer

Sign in at your dashboard and press Delete my account (top right). You'll confirm with your password, and we immediately erase your login, connected Instagram accounts and access token, all keyword rules, conversations, collected emails and sessions. Nothing is kept except anonymised visit counters.

2. I got a DM from a ReplyFlow-powered account

We store the commenter ID, comment ID and any message you replied with for that business account. To have it removed, reply to the business's DM asking them to delete it, or email us with the Instagram handle of the account that messaged you and we'll remove the associated rows for you.

3. Automatic deletion via Meta

If you revoke ReplyFlow's permissions in Instagram settings, or delete your Instagram account, Meta calls our data-deletion / deauthorize endpoints and we drop the stored access token and any data tied to your user id right away.

Contact

Email privacy@replyflow.app with the Instagram handle or email address involved. We confirm once deletion is complete.
